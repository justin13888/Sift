! Scope: resources whose only purpose is reporting that a message was opened. Remote
! content is already blocked by default (FR-8), so these rules decide what still stays
! blocked **after** a user allows a sender: an allowance is consent to the sender's images,
! not to a third party learning when they were looked at.
!
! Prefer a path-anchored rule over a whole host wherever the service also serves content a
! reader wants, so that blocking the report does not also block the message's pictures.
